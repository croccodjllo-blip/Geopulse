public site signals and generating publishable artifacts (llms.txt, JSON-LD, meta, robots).

## Scoring honesty
AIO and GEO scores are heuristic/probe-based diagnostics derived from crawlable signals
(content, structured data, bots policy, llms.txt, technical readiness).
They are not live multi-LLM citation measurements unless a finding is explicitly marked Measured.
See https://geopulse.it/metodologia and https://geopulse.it/guide/score-vs-sov
